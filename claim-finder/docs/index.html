The honest version of "free money": states hold billions in dormant bank accounts, uncashed paychecks, and old deposits — and by law must return them, free, to whoever proves ownership. There is no trick, no bot, and no paid service needed. Beware anyone charging you a % to do what your state does free; a handful of states allow small finder commissions, but you can always file yourself for nothing.
